# 📊 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Description

This project presents an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard for Ferns N Petals (FNP)**, developed using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights by analyzing revenue, orders, customer spending, product performance, occasions, cities, delivery trends, and order-time patterns.

The dashboard uses **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, and interactive Slicers** to provide a dynamic view of sales performance and help identify key business trends and opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze FNP's sales data and answer important business questions such as:

- How much total revenue is generated?
- What is the total number of orders?
- Which occasions generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories perform the best?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which cities have the highest number of orders?
- How does revenue change across different months?
- During which hours are customers placing high-value orders?
- What is the average customer spending?
- What is the average delivery time?

The analysis is designed to support **data-driven decision-making** across sales, marketing, inventory, and operations.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Revenue by Occasion

The dashboard analyzes revenue generated across different occasions, including:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Diwali
- Holi
- Raksha Bandhan
- Valentine's Day

### Key Insight

**Anniversary** and **Raksha Bandhan** are among the strongest revenue-generating occasions, highlighting the importance of occasion-based gifting demand.

This insight can help the business plan seasonal campaigns, promotional offers, and inventory allocation around high-performing occasions.

---

### 2. Revenue by Product Category

The dashboard compares revenue across major product categories:

- Colors
- Mugs
- Raksha Bandhan
- Soft Toys
- Sweets

### Key Insight

**Soft Toys** generate the highest revenue among the displayed categories, followed by Sweets and Colors.

This indicates strong customer demand for gifting products and provides an opportunity to focus marketing and inventory efforts on high-performing categories.

---

### 3. Top 10 Cities by Orders

The dashboard identifies the cities contributing the highest number of orders.

### Key Insight

**Kavali** records the highest number of orders among the displayed cities, followed by cities such as Machilipatnam and Bharathpur.

This analysis can help identify high-potential markets and support city-level marketing and delivery planning.

---

### 4. Revenue by Month

Monthly revenue analysis shows fluctuations in sales performance throughout the year.

A significant revenue peak can be observed in **February**, while April records comparatively lower revenue before sales recover in the following months.

### Business Implication

Monthly trends can help the business:

- Forecast seasonal demand
- Plan inventory in advance
- Optimize marketing campaigns
- Allocate delivery resources
- Identify low-performing periods and create targeted promotions

---

### 5. Top 5 Products by Revenue

The dashboard identifies the five products generating the highest revenue.

Among the displayed products, the **Magnam Set** is the highest revenue-generating product.

### Business Implication

High-performing products can be prioritized for:

- Inventory planning
- Promotional campaigns
- Cross-selling
- Product recommendations
- Revenue growth strategies

---

### 6. Revenue by Hour

The dashboard analyzes revenue based on the time at which orders are placed.

The analysis shows noticeable revenue peaks during the **evening hours**, particularly around **6 PM–7 PM**.

### Business Implication

Understanding peak ordering hours can help optimize:

- Delivery workforce allocation
- Order processing
- Customer support
- Digital marketing campaigns
- Promotional timing

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the analysis, the dashboard provides the following insights:

1. The business generated **₹5.86 lakh in revenue from 126 orders**.

2. The average customer spend is approximately **₹4,652**, indicating a relatively high average order value.

3. **Soft Toys** are the leading revenue-generating product category among the categories analyzed.

4. **Anniversary** and **Raksha Bandhan** are important revenue-generating occasions.

5. **Kavali** records the highest order volume among the displayed cities.

6. Revenue shows noticeable **seasonal fluctuations**, with February being one of the strongest months.

7. The **Magnam Set** is the top revenue-generating product among the displayed top five products.

8. Revenue shows strong activity during **evening ordering hours**, providing an opportunity to optimize operations around peak demand.

9. The average delivery time is approximately **5.72 days**, which can be used as an operational KPI for monitoring and improving delivery performance.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Occasions

Increase marketing campaigns and promotional activities around high-revenue occasions such as Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan.

### 2. Strengthen High-Performing Categories

Maintain sufficient inventory and improve product visibility for high-performing categories such as Soft Toys.

### 3. Implement City-Level Marketing

Use city-level order trends to identify high-potential markets and develop localized marketing strategies.

### 4. Optimize Peak Ordering Hours

Allocate additional operational and delivery resources during high-demand evening hours.

### 5. Improve Delivery Efficiency

Track delivery performance and identify opportunities to reduce the average delivery time, improving customer satisfaction.

### 6. Plan Inventory Based on Seasonal Trends

Use historical monthly revenue patterns to forecast demand and prepare inventory before expected sales peaks.
